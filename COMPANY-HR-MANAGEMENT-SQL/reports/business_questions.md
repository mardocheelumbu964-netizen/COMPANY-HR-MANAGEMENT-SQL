# HR Business Questions

## Employee Analytics
1. What is the total number of employees?
2. What is the headcount by department?
3. Which employees are managers?
4. Which employees have no project assignment?
5. What is the average employee tenure?

## Salary Analytics
6. Who are the top 10 highest-paid employees?
7. What is the average salary by department?
8. Which employees earn above their department average?
9. What department has the highest salary cost?
10. Which employees received the largest salary increases?

## Attendance & Leave
11. Which employees have the most absences?
12. Which departments have the highest overtime?
13. How many leave requests are approved or pending?
14. Which employees have repeated leave requests?

## Performance
15. Who are the top performers?
16. Which employees have ratings below 4.0?
17. What is the average performance rating by department?

## Skills
18. Which skills are most common?
19. Which employees have SQL skills?
20. Which skills have the largest gap for the company?

## Projects
21. Which projects have the highest budgets?
22. Which projects have the most employees?
23. Which employees work on multiple projects?
24. Which departments manage the most projects?

## Payroll
25. What is total gross payroll?
26. What is total net payroll?
27. What is total deduction?
28. Which department has the highest payroll?
